## Why this matters

Reports open with a full-page cover when the front matter says `cover: full`. Add `contents: yes` for a contents page built from the section headings.

## How it works

The cover uses the template's logo, colors and accent, so every template gets its own look.

## Using it

Fields: title, kicker, tagline (or subtitle), byline, author, version and date.
